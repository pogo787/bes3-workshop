Document.

Connect your eBike via USB-C or Web Bluetooth. BES3 Workshop reads component and diagnostic data, streams the BLE live telemetry interface, writes the bike light, service interval and System ID registers, and exports a multi-page PDF diagnostic report. Every write is re-read from the bike before it is reported as verified.

Bluetooth tip: for the most reliable connection, pair the eBike in your operating system's Bluetooth settings before clicking Connect. On Windows, open Bluetooth & devices and add the eBike (it appears as smart system eBike).

or – offline, no bike required

WebUSB unavailable. Use Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS or Linux. Demo and saved readouts still work.
